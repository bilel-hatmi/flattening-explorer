# Long prose for /projects/well-being-panel.
---

A summer research internship at Elements Impact on the Boussole project, under Emmanuelle Bioud (PhD, cognitive science).

The work started upstream of any model: reviewing subjective well-being indicators and translating them into operational variables and predictive targets that an impact assessment could use. Then supervised models were trained, evaluated and selected on a twenty-year longitudinal panel of more than a thousand individuals, with feature engineering, cross-validation, sensitivity analyses and scenario stress-tests of how stable the measurement is over time.

What it taught: signal quality in large-scale behavioural measurement is often weak, because the measurements are heterogeneous, noisy and sensitive to definitional choices. Most of the work is deciding what the number means before estimating it.
