# /projects/eleven-agents — Eleven Strategy, 2025. From Bilel's CV bullets.
---

<p class="lead">A series of AI agents, built as proofs of concept for the firm's clients, to automate the flow of work between analysts and investors.</p>

<dl class="work">
<dt>Deep research for an investment fund</dt>
<dd>A function that researches a company or a market and returns a structured analysis, in the format the fund's analysts already use.</dd>
<dt>CRM for the fund's portfolio</dt>
<dd>A function that manages the portfolio's relationships from inside the client's own environment: mail, marketing, prospects.</dd>
<dt>Client files at a large private bank</dt>
<dd>A function that processes client files automatically, from intake to the elements a banker needs to decide.</dd>
</dl>

Across the proofs of concept, analyst processing time fell by about half and the number of files handled per month doubled.
