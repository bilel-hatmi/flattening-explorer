# Long prose for /projects/eleven-strategy.
---

Six months as an analyst and data scientist in a Paris strategy consultancy, split between client work and the firm's own tooling.

On the client side: buy-side due diligence on two B2C targets (automotive after-sales; the freelancer platform market), with market sizing, competitor benchmarking and business-plan support. The recommendations were used by senior management after the deal.

On the tooling side: AI agents prototyped for company research and CRM pre-processing, cutting analyst processing time by about half, and an internal semantic search system built end to end (embedding pipeline, metadata-aware indexing, query preprocessing, a lightweight retrieval evaluation), which doubled retrieval precision on the firm's benchmarks and reached about eighty active users.

The lasting observation is the one that became *The Flattening*: throughput and decision quality were measured separately, and they routinely pointed in different directions.
