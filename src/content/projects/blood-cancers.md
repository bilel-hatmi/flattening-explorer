# Long prose for /projects/blood-cancers.
---

Eighteen months as a research assistant at the MICS laboratory of CentraleSupélec, on mathematical modelling for health, in collaboration with the Gustave Roussy Institute.

The object was myeloproliferative syndromes, a family of blood cancers that begin with mutated haematopoietic stem cells. The existing model followed the clonal expansion of heterozygous mutated cells. Our contribution was to extend it: through homologous recombination, heterozygous cells can become homozygous and seed a second clone with its own dynamics. Simulating the extended model required the law of the appearance of the first homozygous cells, for which three approaches were compared. Bayesian calibration then gave a model-based distribution for the age at which preferential screening is worth doing, under uncertainty.

The same period covered a first project on self-exciting point processes, which grew into the Hawkes estimation work.
