<p class="lead">When people hand the routine to the machine, they lose the know-how and, with it, the judgment that would have caught its errors. The remedy proposed here is <strong>a tutor that measures what the learner knows before it answers</strong>.</p>

## Where it stands

**2 students**, from 4 August to 28 September 2026. **The structural model and its metacognitive extension come first**; the experiment and a proof of concept follow if time allows, the theory having proved more complex than anticipated. The project deck is linked above.
