<p class="lead">People leave a conflict unresolved because <strong>resolving it would expose what they keep hidden</strong>, more often than because a fix is missing. The project derives a game-theoretic model, adapted to language models, for resolving conflicts under confidentiality constraints.</p>

## Where it stands

**2 students** have worked on it since 4 August. **The theory came first** and proved more complex than anticipated: discovering the right game, transferring its properties and theorems, then deriving the mediator under parsimony. The agentic workflow and the instantiation test follow, with a reduced experimental scope, before the stream closes on 28 September 2026. The project deck is linked above.
