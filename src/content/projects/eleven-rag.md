<p class="lead">Eleven Strategy is a strategy consulting firm advising senior decision-makers on digital transformation and growth. Its internal chat needed to answer from the firm's own documents; <strong>I built the search engine behind it</strong>.</p>

The pipeline embeds all internal documents, with the metadata embedded separately so that retrieval stays precise on dates, clients and document types. Queries are preprocessed before retrieval, and a lightweight evaluation measures the quality of the answers on a benchmark of real questions from the firm.

**On that benchmark, retrieval precision doubled. About 80 people across the firm use it.**

<p class="sources"><a href="/projects/eleven-agents">The AI agents</a> come from the same internship. Its strategy side, 2 buy-side due diligences and a competitive map of bike-sharing in Paris, is on the <a href="/journey">journey</a> page.</p>
