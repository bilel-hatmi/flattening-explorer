## Some maths

The proximal identification argument rests on a bridge function $h$ solving an integral equation of the first kind,

$$
\mathbb{E}\big[\,Y - h(W, A, X)\;\big|\; Z, A, X\,\big] = 0,
$$

which is the kind of object the dissertation deals with. Inline, one writes $\theta = \mathbb{E}[h(W,1,X) - h(W,0,X)]$ and moves on.
